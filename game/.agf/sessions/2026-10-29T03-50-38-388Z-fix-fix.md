### 1. Level map path drew blank on high-density phones (fixed)
- **Cause:** `src/ui/LevelMap.tsx` drew the path on one Skia Canvas as tall as the whole map (about 17,200 px at 3.5x), which is over the GPU texture limit.
- **Fix:** the path is now drawn in canvas tiles of at most 2000 dp, each showing its own band of the same path, as in the factory template (commit 4359ff6). Because every tile draws the same path, it should join up at tile edges with no gaps. The nodes are unchanged, so they still sit on the path.

### 2. Policy issue
- **Fixed in the app:** Settings never showed the privacy policy. The link was built but never given a URL. `src/App.tsx` now passes `https://jeet-s.github.io/jeetech-g003/privacy.html`, which loads and names AdMob.
- **Likely cause (needs a factory fix):** that page, the landing page and the terms page all still call the app **"Coming soon"**. The text says `Privacy Policy Coming soon (com.jeetech.g003)`. They were generated when the Play slot was created (2026-10-17) and never updated with the real title, "Minesweeper Offline: Cozy Fields". A privacy policy that doesn't name the app is a common reason Google flags an app under its user data policy.
  - **Where:** the `jeetech-g003` repo's `docs/` pages, generated by `lib/publish/github.ts`.
  - **Suggested fix:** regenerate `docs/index.html`, `privacy.html` and `terms.html` with the game's title when a game is assigned to a slot. Then check the other slots for the same "Coming soon" text.
- **Still to do:** "Changes in review" may only mean a submission is waiting for review. Please open the app's Policy status in Play Console to see what Google actually cites and its deadline. If it's something other than the privacy policy, it needs its own fix.
